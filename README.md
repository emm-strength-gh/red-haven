# Red Haven

A mobile-minded collection of low-poly 3D games and tools, including a colony survival prototype set on a habitable Mars. The colony game has Expedition and Endless modes, construction, automated colonists, gathering, raids, random events, and local saves. The hub also includes Snakes, Tetris, Arrange Numbers, Spell It!, Color Pour, Frostfall, the Body Mechanics pose studio, Tumble Temple, Little Cup Plaza, Toy Cars Racing, Rocket to the Rim, Cube Lab, Commute City, and a GTA 1 mobile player. Most games are available offline through the PWA; the GTA browser engine needs a network connection.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL printed by Vite. For a production build, run `npm run build`. The static output is in `dist/`. Push the repository to GitHub with a `main` branch, then select **GitHub Actions** as the Pages source in the repository settings. The included workflow builds and deploys `dist/` on each push to `main`. The Vite base is relative, so project subpaths work. GitHub Pages provides HTTPS for PWA installation and offline caching.

You can also play Spell It! by double clicking `spell.html`. It opens the included `spell-local.html`, which works from a local folder. After changing the spelling game, run `npm run build:spell-local` to refresh that copy.

## Controls

- In GTA 1 Mobile, transfer a ZIP of your own original GTA `GTADATA` folder to your phone. The Red Haven page opens GTA.js in a separate tab because GTA.js refuses cross-site embedding. Choose **+ Add .zip** there, select your ZIP, then choose the new data source and play. The engine provides touch controls. GTA.js is an independent third-party reimplementation loaded over the internet; its behavior can differ from the original. No GTA files are included in this repository or its GitHub Pages build.

- On the landing page, choose a game. Arrange Numbers lets you enter a whole-number range from 0–9999, with up to 60 blocks per round.
- In Arrange Numbers, tap blocks in ascending order. Correct blocks roll to the wall; wrong blocks burst and reappear in a new free spot. Drag or wheel-scroll to explore, pinch or use +/− to zoom, and use Center to reset the view. The Mars scene has 15 rotating background events; the comet button starts the next one. Sound effects can be muted.
- In Spell It!, look at the picture on the billboard and tap the letter blocks in order; correct letters roll onto the shelf, wrong ones burst and come back. A faint ghost cube on the shelf shows the next letter and disappears when the word is complete. The word is read aloud when finished. Settings (from the menu) turn words on or off, add your own words with a picture, and change sound, voice, hints, the ghost cube and the number of extra letters. 15 gentle Mars sky events happen in the background.
- In Color Pour, tap a bottle and then a compatible bottle to pour its top color. Swipe left or right to circle the board, pinch or use +/− (or the mouse wheel) to zoom. Each level starts as a solvable scramble; complete every single-color bottle to unlock the next stop on the scrollable Martian level map. Use Undo or Restart as needed. Stars, unlocked levels, and the sound setting are saved locally. The game has synthesized tap, pour, undo, and win sounds and supports number keys 1–9 for bottle selection.
- In Frostfall, walk your astronaut around an isometric Martian polar outpost (drag anywhere, WASD/arrows or tap the ground). Walk up to ice crystals, ore rocks and carbon rocks to gather ice, metal and fuel. The Heat Core burns fuel to keep the warm zone heated; outside it you get cold. Use Build to place pumps, drills, fuel stills, habitats, a workshop, ice walls, blaster turrets and tesla coils, and tap any building to upgrade it (the Heat Core level caps everything else). Recruit helper bots that gather on their own, research upgrades in the Workshop, grab supply drops, and survive blizzards and crawler waves every night. Progress saves locally.
- In Body Mechanics, drag the colorful controls around a low-poly mannequin or use precise joint sliders. Human-inspired limits prevent impossible joint rotations. Adjust torso, femur, shin, arm and other proportions; load strength-training presets; save reusable poses locally; and blend any number of saved poses into a looping animation.
- In Tumble Temple, swipe, use the arrow keys/WASD or the on-screen pad to flip a stone block end over end across floating gothic ruins, and stand it upright in the glowing pit. Three temples hold 22 levels each and add cracked flagstones that crumble behind you, stained glass that shatters under an upright block, and round and cross sigils that raise bridges. Every level is checked by a solver; par is the shortest solution. Press R to restart. Stars and unlocked levels are saved locally. Regenerate the levels with `node tools/gen-tumble-levels.mjs`.
- In Little Cup Plaza, grow one little café into a whole shopping mall. Drag to paint mall floor (walls go up on their own), add entrances, footpaths and car parks that link to the sidewalk, then place shop units with their doors facing the concourse (R rotates). Shoppers arrive by car, on foot and later by bus, each with wishes, a budget and needs (restrooms, food, rest). Run shops yourself (staff, prices, stock, upgrades) or lease them to tenants for rent. Hire janitors, security guards and technicians, research new licences (bakery to cinema and bowling) and upgrades in the office, and handle events such as power cuts, leaks, shoplifting rings, inspections and celebrity visits. Wages, upkeep and loan interest are paid at closing, followed by a daily report showing what shoppers wanted. Modes: Normal, Challenge, Expert and Sandbox (unlimited money). Keys: drag/wheel to pan and zoom, Q/E rotate view, Space pause, 1–3 speed. Progress saves locally.
- In Toy Cars Racing, race chunky toy cars across six tracks: Bedroom Blitz (orange plastic track, loop and toy-box tunnel), Neon City (two-way traffic, roadworks, back alley), Canyon Rush (ravine jump, dirt cut), Frosty Summit (mountain pass, ice tunnel, frozen-lake cut), Mega Loop Mayhem (figure-8 bridge, two loops, bridge gap) and Backyard Jungle (giant garden). Race 5 AI rivals on Easy, Normal or Hard, or run Time Trial against a ghost of your best lap. Touch controls: left side steers (slide between ◀ and ▶) with the Nitro button above; right side has Gas, Brake/reverse and Drift (handbrake). Keys: ↑/W gas, ↓/S brake, ←→/AD steer, Space drift, Shift/N nitro, C camera, R reset car, Esc pause. Gamepads work too. The 🎥 button switches between 3rd-person chase, driver POV and bird's-eye views. Drifting, big air and loops refill nitro; boost pads give a burst. The Garage has four cars with different stats and paint colours. Best laps, ghosts, trophies and settings (auto-gas, touch controls, graphics) save locally.
- In Cube Lab, twist a glossy 3D puzzle cube. Drag a sticker to turn its layer (it follows your finger and snaps to the nearest quarter or half turn; a quick flick counts too). Drag the background or use two fingers to spin the cube, and pinch or wheel to zoom. **Puzzles & Levels** has 40 levels in 7 chapters: First Twists (watch a short scramble, then undo it), Pattern Studio (match a target cube shown in the corner, in any orientation — tap it to see the back), Pocket Cube (2×2), Locked Layers (only the labelled faces turn), Beat the Clock, Big Cubes (4×4 and 5×5 inner slices) and Grand Master full scrambles. Par sets the stars; 💡 Hint shows the next turn with a glowing arrow and costs a star. **Free Sandbox** offers 2×2 up to 7×7 with Shuffle, Undo/Redo, an animated auto-Solve, Reset and a solve timer with best times per size. The ⌨ pad and keys turn faces relative to your view: U D L R F B (Shift = reverse), M E S slices, X Y Z spin the cube, Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, Space shuffles in the sandbox. Settings choose Classic, Neon or Pastel cube styles, turn speed, sound and the sandbox timer. Progress saves locally.
- In Commute City, keep a cartoon toy town moving. Drag across tiles to build roads, motorways, tram tracks and railways; the Ground / Flyover / Tunnel switch builds bridges over roads, rivers and motorways or tunnels under mountains and water, and a road that joins a motorway becomes an entrance or exit. Tap a junction with the Lights or Roundabout tool to control it (tap a light in Look mode to switch it by hand), place tram and train stops and draw transit lines between them. **City** has four maps where houses and workplaces of six colours keep growing: link each house to the matching workplace, earn money for every trip and stop any workplace from waiting too long, through day, night and rush hour. **Road Puzzles** are 10 fixed maps with a budget and a par cost for three stars (pieces that come with the map can't be erased). **Signal Levels** are 8 junction challenges: tap each light once to let one car through, twice for green and again for red, avoid crashes, clear level crossings for trains and don't keep drivers waiting. **Sandbox** gives unlimited money, no game over, and lets you place houses and workplaces yourself or turn growth off. Drag to pan, right-drag or two fingers to rotate and tilt, wheel or pinch to zoom; 🔭 / V switches to a top-down view and 🎥 / C rides along with any car, tram or train in chase or driver view. Keys: WASD/arrows pan, Q/E rotate, +/− zoom, 1–0 pick tools, Tab switches Ground / Flyover / Tunnel, Space pauses, Esc drops the tool or leaves a ride. Progress and cities save locally.
- Tap/click a build card, then a highlighted empty tile to place it.
- Tap/click a rock or tree in Gather mode to mark it for harvesting.
- Tap/click a building, colonist, or tile in Inspect mode for details.
- Drag to pan, pinch or wheel to zoom. Use the speed button to pause or accelerate time.
- Saves are stored on this device in browser local storage. New Game replaces the current local save after confirmation.

## First playable scope

The simulation uses a deterministic seeded map, low-poly meshes made from primitives, instanced terrain, a fixed orthographic camera, and a capped active unit count. Expedition mode aims to construct and power a signal beacon while keeping the crew alive. Endless mode has no victory deadline. More advanced job scheduling, research, colony relationships, and multiplayer are future work.
