Play the 1997 top-down Grand Theft Auto on your phone with touch controls. Bring your own game data ZIP to unlock the full game.

Launch GTA.js player
01Move a ZIP of your GTADATA folder to your phone.
02Launch the player, then choose + Add .zip.
03Choose a city and play. Touch controls appear in the game.

The game opens on GTA.js because that engine does not allow other sites to embed it. Internet is needed to load it. Your GTA data is selected there; Red Haven does not host it. GTA.js is an independent reimplementation and can differ from the original game.
