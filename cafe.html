The Journey

How to run a café

1 · Plan the day

Before opening, use the clipboard: pick drinks and prices on the Menu, stock the Pantry, hire your Team, and Decorate. The forecast warns you about events and possible troubles.

2 · Open the doors

Flip the sign. Guests line up at the register — tap them (or press Take order) and their ticket clips onto the rail.

3 · Make the drinks

Tap a ticket, then tap the recipe steps at the bar. For ☕ shots and ♨️ milk, stop the needle in the green. Press Serve. Keys: 1–9 for steps, Space to serve.

4 · Keep guests happy

Short waits, correct orders, fair prices and cozy décor raise happiness and tips. Very happy guests may become regulars.

5 · Layout matters

A seat must be next to a table to count. Keep the line and door clear. Plants, lamps and shelves add comfort.

6 · Grow

Earn reputation to unlock décor, equipment and expansions: patios, lofts, takeaway windows. Finish a café’s shifts to travel to the next.

Troubles

Jammed machines, late deliveries, rushes, sick staff, spills and shortages come with choices. Pay to fix them, change the menu, move staff around, or take a small hit.
