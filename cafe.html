Visitor Guide

🏗️ Build

Paint mall floor by dragging. Walls go up around the edge on their own. Add entrances on the outer edge, then footpaths and car parks that touch the sidewalk.

🏬 Open shops

Drop shop units on the floor with their door facing the concourse (R rotates). Run them yourself (staff, prices, stock) or lease them for steady rent.

🚶 Shoppers

Shoppers arrive by car, on foot and by bus. They want shops, food, restrooms and benches. Click anyone to read their thoughts. Empty wishes show up in Reports.

🔬 Research

Licences unlock new kinds of shops and upgrades. A Management Office speeds research up a lot.

👷 Staff & trouble

Janitors sweep litter, guards catch shoplifters, technicians fix breakdowns. Events pop up with choices. Pick wisely.

💰 Money

Wages, stock and upkeep are paid at closing time. Take a loan if you need one, but interest adds up. Fall too far into debt and the bank takes the keys.
